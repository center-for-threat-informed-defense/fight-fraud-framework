## Phishing technique restructuring

Replaced the ATT&CK-derived phishing structures with an F3-native technique family that covers phishing used to gather information or obtain access during fraud activity.

### Removed from the active matrix

- Removed T1660, the ATT&CK Mobile Phishing technique, earlier in the v1.3 drafting process.
- Removed T1566, Phishing, and T1566.001 through T1566.004.
- Removed T1598, Phishing for Information, and T1598.001 through T1598.004.
- Historical versions retain the removed ATT&CK-derived techniques.

### Added F1069 — Phishing

| Technique ID | Technique | Tactics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1069 | Phishing | Initial Access; Reconnaissance |
| F1069.001 | Phishing: Vishing | Initial Access; Reconnaissance |
| F1069.002 | Phishing: Smishing | Initial Access; Reconnaissance |
| F1069.003 | Phishing: Quishing | Initial Access; Reconnaissance |
| F1069.004 | Phishing: Phishing Link | Initial Access; Reconnaissance |

The new definitions cover voice calls, mobile text messages, QR codes, and links delivered through email, social media, messaging applications, collaboration platforms, advertisements, compromised websites, and other electronic communications. They include natural inline links to related F3 behaviors involving impersonation, sender and caller spoofing, fraudulent websites and applications, remote-access software, fraudulent call centers, authentication requests, account changes, synthetic voices, and victim-authorized transfers.

F1069 and its four sub-techniques received Added Date and Modified Date values of 2026-11-18.

## Online persona technique restructuring

### Replaced

- Removed T1585, Establish Accounts.
- Removed T1585.004, Establish Accounts: Dating App Accounts.
- Replaced the ATT&CK-derived account-establishment structure with an F3-native technique family focused on creating and cultivating online personas for fraud.

### Added under F1060 — Create Online Persona

| Technique ID | Technique | Tactic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1060 | Create Online Persona | Resource Development |
| F1060.001 | Create Online Persona: Dating App Account | Resource Development |
| F1060.002 | Create Online Persona: Social Media Account | Resource Development |

The Social Media Account definition incorporates concepts from ATT&CK T1585.001, including persona history, profile information, photographs, social connections, affiliations, and use across one or more services, with the language adapted to fraud activity. T1586.001, Compromise Accounts: Social Media Accounts, remains unchanged and continues to cover the compromise of an existing account rather than the creation of a new persona.

F1060 and its two sub-techniques received Added Date and Modified Date values of 2026-11-18.

## Financial account technique restructuring

### Modified F1021 — Open Financial Account

- Renamed F1021 from Create Fraudulent Merchant Account to Open Financial Account.
- Expanded the parent definition to cover actor-controlled financial accounts opened to receive illicit funds or conduct fraudulent transactions.
- Retained Resource Development as the tactic.
- Retained the existing Added Date and set the Modified Date to 2026-11-18.

### Added under F1021 — Open Financial Account

| Technique ID | Technique | Tactic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1021.001 | Open Financial Account: Open Merchant Account | Resource Development |
| F1021.002 | Open Financial Account: Open Consumer Account | Resource Development |

The merchant-account definition retains applicable behavior from the former F1021 definition. The consumer-account definition covers accounts opened for first-party or third-party fraud, including the creation of credentials for the financial institution's website or mobile application and depositing a bad check before withdrawing or spending provisionally credited funds.

F1021.001 and F1021.002 received Added Date and Modified Date values of 2026-11-18.

## Securities and transaction-processing techniques

Added three F3-native techniques covering securities fraud and exploitation of transaction-processing gaps.

| Technique ID | Technique | Tactics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1062 | Sale of Securities | Monetization |
| F1063 | Artificial Inflation of Securities | Execution |
| F1064 | Exploit Transaction Processing Gaps | Positioning |

F1062 and F1063 contain reciprocal links between artificial price or demand inflation and the subsequent sale of securities. F1064 covers timing and control gaps involving authorization, posting, provisional credit, clearing, settlement, reconciliation, reversals, batch processing, system synchronization, and offline ATM or payment-network activity.

F1062, F1063, and F1064 received Added Date and Modified Date values of 2026-11-18. F1064 removed Monetization and retained Positioning before release.

## Identity, control-bypass, and mule-recruitment techniques

Added three F3-native techniques covering synthetic identity creation, general security-control bypass behavior, and recruitment of money mules.

| Technique ID | Technique | Tactic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1065 | Create Synthetic Identity | Resource Development |
| F1066 | Bypass Controls | Stealth |
| F1067 | Mule Recruitment | Resource Development |

F1065 links the use of synthetic identities to F1021 Open Financial Account, F1047 Transfer of Funds, and F1049 Use Money Mule. F1067 includes inline FBI and Recorded Future references and links follow-on mule activity to F1049 Use Money Mule.

F1065, F1066, and F1067 received Added Date and Modified Date values of 2026-11-18. F1066 was renamed from Bypass Security Controls to Bypass Controls and its definition was narrowed to generalized avoidance of a control's decision or enforcement point. The revision distinguishes it from specific Stealth behaviors such as spoofing, impersonation, structuring, and indicator removal.

## Modified F1049 — Use Money Mule

- Retained Positioning and Stealth as the technique’s tactics.
- Expanded the definition to cover mule-controlled accounts, cards, wallets, payment instruments, identities, devices, and businesses.
- Added receiving, holding, transferring, withdrawing, spending, transporting, converting, purchasing, delivery, resale, funnel-account, and cash-out behaviors.
- Added inline FBI and Recorded Future references.
- Added links to F1021 Open Financial Account, F1009 Bank Deposit, and F1047 Transfer of Funds.
- Retained the existing Added Date and set the Modified Date to 2026-11-18.

## Transfer of Funds restructuring

Reorganized the Transfer of Funds family around the payment mechanism used to move funds. The parent and all four subtechniques retain Monetization and Execution.

### Deprecated or retired

- Deprecated F1025, Electronic Funds Transfer, and F1025.001 through F1025.003. Historical versions retain the removed technique family.
- Retired the names Transfer of Funds: Victim-Authorized Transfer and Transfer of Funds: Unauthorized Transfer.
- Retained the F1047.001 and F1047.002 identifiers while assigning them new names and definitions.

### Updated Transfer of Funds family

| Technique ID | Technique | Tactics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1047 | Transfer of Funds | Monetization; Execution |
| F1047.001 | Transfer of Funds: Peer-to-Peer Transfer | Monetization; Execution |
| F1047.002 | Transfer of Funds: Real-Time Payment | Monetization; Execution |
| F1047.003 | Transfer of Funds: Regional Payment Rail | Monetization; Execution |
| F1047.004 | Transfer of Funds: Wire Transfer | Monetization; Execution |

The Peer-to-Peer Transfer, Regional Payment Rail, and Wire Transfer definitions reuse and refine applicable language from the deprecated F1025 family. Real-Time Payment was added to cover systems that make funds available within seconds or minutes, often continuously and with limited opportunity to stop or reverse an accepted payment.

F1047 retained its existing Added Date value and received a Modified Date of 2026-11-18. F1047.001 and F1047.002 retained their Added Dates of 2026-08-21 and received Modified Dates of 2026-11-18. F1047.003 and F1047.004 received Added Date and Modified Date values of 2026-11-18.

## Added F1068 — Call Forwarding

Added Call Forwarding as an F3-native technique covering the deceptive activation of telephone call forwarding to redirect a victim's incoming calls to an actor-controlled destination. The technique includes interception of authentication and account-recovery communications, impersonation of the victim when receiving calls from institutions or service providers, and suppression of fraud alerts.

| Technique ID | Technique | Tactics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1068 | Call Forwarding | Initial Access; Defense Impairment |

F1068 received Added Date and Modified Date values of 2026-11-18.
